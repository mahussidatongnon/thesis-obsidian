## Context
Digital Twins is the digital representation of an original system with a two directional data / informations flow.  These include models based on knowledge (deductive models) and those based on data (inductive models). Therefore a natural place where both data based and knowledge based model coexists and are hybridized.
## Problem
    Hybridization that actually happens in many DT is often not well documented and done in an ad-hoc way to achieved functional goal. It makes difficult to understand the goal.
## Approach
    - The paper proposes to details about the engineering approach taken in developing the two DT where hybridization is realized at different lifecycle phases of model (creation, usage, update) and at different lifecycle of the DT. 
    - Additionally, the paper elaborates on a way to systematize the description of model hybridization in DT.
## Result
Results show that hybridization suffers a lack of systematic description, by structuring the way the hybridization is conducted, it helps in a better understanding of the role, benefits and drawback of hybridization.
