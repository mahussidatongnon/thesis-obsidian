# Short papers
## Vision and New Ideas
- clearly explain the challenge, 
- describe an engineering-based solution idea,
- explain the difference to the current state of the art 
- the expected benefits of realizing the solution idea.

## Exemplars
- must include details about the engineering approach taken in developing the DT (details of how the DT was developed)
- lessons learned that have the potential of being transferred to other DT engineering projects.
- come with an appendix with screenshots and / or a short video / screencast illustrating the DT (possibly providing links to online material or further information about the DT.)

## Tool demonstration papers
- practical insights that are likely to be useful to other implementers or users of DTs in the future
- must include an appendix (which will not be published) with a demo outline / screenshots and/or a short video/screencast illustrating the tool, as well as possibly providing links to online material. (*for review purpose*)
